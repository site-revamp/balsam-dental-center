# Balsam Clinics – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

**Balsam Clinics** is a dental healthcare provider based in Dubai, UAE, offering general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a professional, modern and patient-centred experience. It presents the clinic's dental services, introduces its practitioners and helps patients navigate treatment options more easily.

The design direction emphasizes a clean visual identity, clear content hierarchy and accessible appointment-focused navigation.

## 2. Main Improvements

Three key improvements were implemented to address issues identified in the original website.

### 1. Brand Identity and Consistency

**Previous issue:** The original website used inconsistent business names, including Balsam Clinics, Balsam Dental and Balsam Dental Clinic. An incorrect Sora Clinic reference also appeared in the footer.

**Implemented fix:**
- Standardized the business identity as Balsam Clinics.
- Applied consistent branding across navigation, hero content, page sections, calls to action and footer.
- Introduced a clean, restrained wordmark approach pending availability of the original logo asset.

**Result:** A more consistent brand presentation throughout the homepage.

### 2. Treatment Discovery and Service Organization

**Previous issue:** The original homepage displayed a long list of dental treatments, making it difficult for visitors to identify relevant services.

**Implemented fix:**
- Organized treatments into five patient pathways: Maintain, Restore, Align, Enhance and Urgent Care.
- Introduced six service cards to improve treatment discovery.
- Structured service information into clearer categories.

**Result:** Visitors can understand the available treatment options and navigate toward relevant services more easily.

### 3. Practitioner Credibility and Patient Trust

**Previous issue:** Practitioner information and clinical credibility received limited prominence on the original homepage.

**Implemented fix:**
- Added a dedicated practitioner section featuring the four identified dental professionals.
- Introduced clearer treatment information.
- Added a three-step patient journey to explain the treatment process.
- Included patient feedback and an FAQ section.
- Avoided unsupported qualifications and clinical claims.

**Result:** The homepage provides greater visibility into the clinic's team, services and patient experience.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces several visual and structural improvements:

- **Visual presentation:** A cleaner, more professional dental website appearance with consistent branding.
- **Layout:** Better content organization and a clearer hierarchy between the hero, treatment pathways, service cards and supporting sections.
- **User experience:** More straightforward navigation through treatment categories and clearer appointment-focused calls to action.
- **Consistency:** Unified business naming, typography and visual presentation across homepage sections.
- **Content structure:** Dedicated sections for practitioners, patient feedback, the treatment journey and frequently asked questions.
- **Overall appearance:** A more organized and cohesive homepage that communicates the clinic's services and identity more clearly.

## 4. Project Scope

The redesign focuses on the homepage structure, visual presentation, service discovery and patient-facing information.

The implementation uses the supplied company research as its content reference. Unverified historical claims, practitioner qualifications and unsupported business information have not been introduced.
