Common questions

Who treats my braces or aligners?

Orthodontic treatment is led by Dr. Haitham Al Juboori, an orthodontist and the clinic’s founder.

Can I pay in instalments?

Tabby and Tamara instalment plans are listed by the clinic, and insurance is accepted. Confirm your plan when booking.

What does the first visit include?

An assessment with the scans or X-rays your case needs, followed by a plan and price. Specialist assessment is AED 590.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We offer children’s cleaning, fillings, early orthodontics and optional nitrous oxide sedation.
